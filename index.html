About

I am an Electrical and Computer Engineering student at the University of Toronto with interests in digital verification, FPGA systems, computer architecture, and machine learning.

My experience includes ASIC digital verification, FPGA-based systems, embedded development, and machine learning. I enjoy working at the intersection of hardware and software, particularly on problems involving digital systems, computation, and hardware acceleration.

I have worked with SystemVerilog and UVM for digital verification, as well as C++, Python, Verilog, and Assembly for software and hardware projects. I am particularly interested in building reliable hardware systems and understanding how computational workloads can be efficiently mapped onto hardware.
